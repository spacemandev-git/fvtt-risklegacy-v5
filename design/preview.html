Do & don't

Do — keep one red display headline per view; set body in Roboto Condensed at 14px; let the marker font annotate (names, flavour, modifiers) rather than explain rules; reserve brackets for real empty slots that a later unlock will fill.
Don't — put red text on concrete or gunmetal (1.0:1); stencil whole paragraphs; round corners on anything but physical cards; recolour Foundry's core UI; use the RISK / LEGACY logotypes (trademarks, not part of this system).
